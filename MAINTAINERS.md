## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Review and merge pull requests
- Triage issues and bug reports
- Manage releases and changelogs
- Participate in architectural discussions
- Enforce the Code of Conduct

## Becoming a Maintainer

Trusted contributors who demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions may be invited to become maintainers. The process:

1. Consistent contributions over 3+ months
2. Nomination by an existing maintainer
3. Approval by the Project Maintainer
4. Signing of the CLA (if not already done)

## Maintainer Limitations

Maintainers do **not** have authority over:
- Licensing decisions or commercial agreements
- Trademark usage or enforcement
- Transfer or sale of project ownership
- Appointment or removal of the Project Maintainer

These powers are reserved exclusively for the Project Maintainer to ensure clean IP ownership for potential acquisition or commercialization.
